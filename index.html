Article 50(1) is framed as a provider obligation, but in practice it's deployers — the businesses running the AI system — who publish this notice to their own users, except where it's already obvious from context that they're talking to AI. This tool covers that limited-risk disclosure only; it doesn't cover Art. 50(3)/50(4) (biometric or emotion-recognition systems, deepfakes, AI-generated text on matters of public interest), high-risk classification, technical documentation, or Member State-specific guidance.
